Privacy Notice

What we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

D7 Solutions runs this website under its own accounts, hosted on Cloudflare. This notice describes what the site collects and what happens to it. It is written to be accurate rather than exhaustive; if something here is unclear, email rob@d7sol.com and we will answer plainly.

What we collect

Information you send us. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you type, plus any optional company, phone, project, and topic you choose to add. That is the only form on this site. With each submission we also record the IP address it came from and the browser's user-agent string, which we use only to block abuse of the form.

Basic request logs. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, records each request (the page requested, the time, the browser's user-agent string, and the IP address) as any web host does. These logs exist so the site can be kept running and problems diagnosed.

What we do with it

We use what you send us to answer you. A message about a lunch and learn goes to whichever of us covers your territory; a message about a product goes to whoever knows that line.

Contact submissions are stored in a database in our Cloudflare account. To reach us quickly, a copy of each submission is sent to our email through a transactional email provider acting on our instructions.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not add you to a marketing list because you asked a technical question. There is no newsletter signup on this site.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or analytics trackers. This site loads no third-party scripts, no ad pixels, and no analytics service.
  • No cookies for tracking. The site does not set cookies to follow you.
  • No third-party fonts or CDNs. Every asset is served from our own domain, through Cloudflare, so no other company sees your visit.

Manufacturers we represent

If your inquiry concerns a specific manufacturer's product, we may need to pass relevant details to that manufacturer to get you an answer, a quote, or warranty support. That is the ordinary work of a representative firm. We share what the question requires and no more.

How long we keep it

Contact submissions are kept as business records for as long as the inquiry and any resulting project relationship are live. Request logs are kept by our host on a short rolling basis for operational purposes.

Your choices

Email rob@d7sol.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will do so unless we are required to keep it as a business or contract record.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Contact submissions are stored on Cloudflare systems under accounts we control. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise — please do not send confidential project documents or anything sensitive through the website contact form. Email or call us instead.

Children

This site is a business-to-business resource for design and construction professionals. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date below.
