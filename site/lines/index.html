Our Lines

Five trusted manufacturers

Each line covers one lane of the envelope. We back all of them with local technical support, submittals, and service — from design assist through installation.

Building Envelope & Waterproofing

Soprema

Soprema is D7's building-envelope and waterproofing line — the weather-control layers that keep air and water out of the assembly. SBS-modified bitumen air and vapor barriers, self-adhered and torch-applied below-grade waterproofing, and the ALSAN family of liquid-applied membranes for details, decks and restoration.

Territory: North Dakota, South Dakota, Minnesota, Wisconsin, Iowa, Nebraska

Wall Insulation

Atlas EnergyShield

Atlas EnergyShield is D7's polyiso continuous-insulation line for walls. Foil- and coated-glass-faced polyiso boards that carry the continuous-insulation R-value the energy code asks for, detailed to work with the air barrier rather than against it.

Territory: North Dakota, South Dakota, Minnesota, Iowa, Nebraska

Concrete Admixtures

Anti-Hydro

Anti-Hydro is D7's integral concrete admixture line — waterproofing and protection dosed into the mix rather than applied to the surface afterward. Water-resisting, corrosion-inhibiting and shrinkage-compensating admixtures for structural and architectural concrete.

Territory: North Dakota, South Dakota, Minnesota, Iowa, Nebraska

Concrete Chemistry & Precast

Nox-Crete

Nox-Crete is D7's concrete chemistry line for the plant and the pour — form-release agents, cures and sealers, bond breakers, and the TigerLoc system for precast and tilt-up work.

Territory: North Dakota, South Dakota, Minnesota, Iowa, Nebraska

Traffic Coatings & Joints

LymTal Iso-Flex

LymTal Iso-Flex is D7's traffic-coating and expansion-joint line. Traffic-bearing deck membranes, Iso-Flex expansion joint systems and sealants for parking structures, plazas and other decks that carry wheels as well as weather.

Territory: Iowa, Nebraska
